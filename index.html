About Us

For over 20 years, Artisan Bakery has been serving the community with freshly baked goods made from scratch. We believe in using organic flour, locally sourced ingredients, and traditional baking methods to create breads and pastries that warm your heart.

Our master bakers start early each morning to ensure every loaf and pastry is baked to perfection when you arrive.
